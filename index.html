Background

Growing Up & Where I'm At

I went to West Windsor-Plainsboro High School North in Plainsboro, NJ, where I spent most of my time doing physical activities, whether that was in the gym or outside with my friends, but I also spent a great deal of time on academics and extracurriculars. I worked as a TA for 3 teachers across my High School career, and developed a keen interest for history and animals as I volunteered at local museum and SAVE, our local animal shelter.

Now I'm studying Computer Engineering & CS at Northeastern and training as an Air Force ROTC cadet. The long-term target is a rated slot — but the short-term one is just getting into the right habits to become a little better everyday.
